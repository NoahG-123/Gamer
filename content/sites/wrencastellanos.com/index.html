I record places. Rooms, shorelines, kitchens at 6 a.m., the inside of a container ship, a nursing home at night. Most of my work is production sound for documentary, and the rest is the libraries below, which are the same recordings that ended up in the films, cleaned and cut so you can use them in yours.

Kit: Sound Devices MixPre-6 II, Sennheiser MKH 8040 pair in ORTF, DPA 4060s, a Zoom F3 for the small stuff, a pair of LOM Uši for when something needs to be very quiet. I mix in REAPER because it does what I tell it.
