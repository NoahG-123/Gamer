Waterfront woman, 21, missing after night out

HALIFAX — Police are asking for the public's help in locating Colleen Anne Mahar, 21, of Barrington Street, who has not been seen since the early hours of Saturday, October 9.

Miss Mahar, a waitress at the Seahorse Tavern, was last seen leaving the establishment at about 1:30 a.m. in the company of two men known to her, both employed on the waterfront. The men told police they drove Miss Mahar to the corner of Barrington and Morris streets and let her out, and that she walked toward home.

Her mother, Mrs. Bernadette Mahar, told the Ledger her daughter "would never leave the baby. Not for a night, not for an hour." Miss Mahar has an infant daughter, eight months old.

Police describe Miss Mahar as five feet four inches, 115 pounds, with dark hair worn long, and say she was wearing a green wool coat, a white blouse, and a gold ring with a small garnet stone which belonged to her mother.

Anyone with information is asked to contact the Halifax Police Department at 426-3311.
