Police: missing Mahar woman "likely went to Toronto"

HALIFAX — Police say they have no evidence of foul play in the disappearance of Colleen Mahar, 21, and believe the young woman may have left the city voluntarily.

"We have spoken to everyone who saw her that night," Det. Sgt. W. Purcell said Monday. "Both gentlemen have been co-operative and their accounts are consistent. There is some indication Miss Mahar had spoken of going to Toronto."

Mrs. Bernadette Mahar, the woman's mother, disputed the suggestion. "She had eleven dollars and a baby. Where is she going to Toronto?"

The two men, Alden Furey, 29, and Gerard Doucette, 30, both of Halifax, were not available for comment. Mr. Furey's employer, the Halifax Longshoremen's Association, said he was "a good worker and a family man."

The file remains open.
