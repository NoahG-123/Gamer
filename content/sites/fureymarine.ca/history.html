Our history

1968

Alden Furey, 26, buys the tug Gerry D. in partnership with his friend Gerard Doucette. The two work the Halifax piers by day and run the tug by night.

1974

Doucette sells his share to Furey after a disagreement. The company is renamed Furey Marine Services.

1989

Derek Furey joins the company after graduating from the Nova Scotia Nautical Institute.

2004

Alden retires. Derek becomes President. The company purchases its second tug and the deck barge Bernadette.

2019

Furey Marine celebrates 50 years with a company that now employs 40 people across two yards.

2026

Alden Furey passes away peacefully at Oakmount Manor in Bedford, surrounded by family. He was 84. Our flag flew at half mast for a month, as it should.
