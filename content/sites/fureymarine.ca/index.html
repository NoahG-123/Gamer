Our history

Founded in 1968 by Alden Furey, a longshoreman who bought a used tug with a bank loan and a handshake, Furey Marine has grown from one boat and two men to a company of forty. Alden's son Derek joined the business in 1989 and has been President since 2004. Alden Furey passed away in July 2026 at the age of 84; the company's Bedford Basin tug, the Alden F., carries his name.
