Slow Rooms

Feature documentary · 92 min (est.) · in post-production · expected 2027

Oakmount Manor sits at the top of a hill in Bedford, Nova Scotia, with 118 beds and a view of the Basin that most of its residents no longer look at. For a year, director Nadia Bishara and a crew of three were given access to the east wing: to its residents, its night staff, its rituals, and its long, ordinary hours.

Slow Rooms is not a film about a crisis. It is a film about what time does when it is allowed to.
